## Full description
Dad Bod is the Dad Physique OS — a premium, completely free fitness companion
for building a lean, strong physique.

DAILY MISSION
Open the app and see your Dad Physique Score — one number that combines
protein, calories, training, hydration, and consistency. Complete the mission,
grow the score, keep the streak.

NUTRITION THAT UNDERSTANDS YOU
• Search-first logging with a 5,200+ item Indian food dataset (works offline)
• Type it like you say it: "200g paneer with 2 roti" — portions are understood
• Online food parsing by Edamam for anything unusual
• Fullscreen barcode scanner with a triple product database
  (Open Food Facts + legacy + UPCItemDB) — Indian products included
• Nutrition-label photo reader (on-device OCR)
• Voice logging, full macros plus 13 micronutrients
• 1,000+ healthy USDA MyPlate Kitchen recipes, loggable in one tap

TRAINING THAT PUSHES YOU
• Structured weekly splits with form cues and rest timers
• Full-screen rest timer with haptics
• Session completion celebrations, PR board with estimated 1RM
• Cardio + abs circuit tracking, steps and sleep burn
• 700+ exercise library with images (wger open database)
• Find gyms and parks near you (OpenStreetMap)

DAD COINS
Earn coins for real behavior — workouts, protein, hydration, weigh-ins,
photos, and streaks (7-day +700, 30-day +5,000). No coins for idle opens.

PROGRESS YOU CAN SEE
• Animated weight trend with goal line
• Before/after photo compare slider
• GitHub-style consistency heatmap
• Strength CSV export, full JSON backup and restore

YOUR ACCOUNT, YOUR KEYS
Sign in with Google to keep your identity and enable encrypted cloud backup, or
continue completely offline, where nothing ever leaves the device. Backups are
encrypted on your phone with a passphrase only you know, so nobody can read
them, including us.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No subscription. No ads. Your data lives on your device —
nutrition and fitness lookups use public APIs with only the text you search.

Built Dream Physique.

## Data safety (Play Console)
- Account (optional): name, email, and profile photo from Google Sign-In, used
  only to identify your profile in-app. Offline profiles collect nothing.
- Cloud backup (optional): an end-to-end encrypted blob the developer cannot
  decrypt, stored under the user's own Firebase UID
- Collects: nothing by default; all logs are on-device
- Location: approximate location used only in-session when the user opens
  "Nearby Gyms & Parks" (not collected, not shared, optional)
- Network requests: food search text (Edamam), barcodes (Open Food Facts),
  profile numbers for USDA calculators (myplate.food), exercise searches
  (wger.de) — no identifiers attached
- Photos: stored locally on device only
- Account: local profile on device (name + email for multi-profile support)

## Upload checklist
1. Build signed AAB: npm run build:android
2. Play Console → Create release → Internal testing → Production
3. Upload DadBod-v2.2.0-signed.aab
4. Add screenshots (phone 1080x2400): Home mission, Diet search, Food sheet,
   Train hero, Rest timer, Progress chart, Photo compare, Control Center
5. Feature graphic 1024x500 (dark aurora + DB logo)
6. App icon 512x512 (export assets/logo-premium.svg to PNG)
7. Data safety form: local-only storage; in-session approximate location
   for Nearby (optional); no data shared or sold
8. Link privacy policy + data deletion pages
9. Target API 36 — already configured
10. AFTER the first upload: copy the Play App Signing SHA-1 from
    Play Console → Setup → App signing, add it to Firebase, re-download
    google-services.json, and run `npm run sync:firebase`. Without this,
    Google Sign-In fails for Play Store installs (error 10).
