## Full description
Dad Bod is the Dad Physique OS — a premium, completely free fitness companion
for building a lean, strong physique.

DAILY MISSION
Open the app and see your Dad Physique Score — one number that combines
protein, calories, training, hydration, and consistency. Complete the mission,
grow the score, keep the streak.

NUTRITION THAT UNDERSTANDS YOU
• Search-first logging with a 5,200+ item Indian food dataset (works offline)
• Type it like you say it: "200g paneer with 2 roti" — portions are understood
• Online food parsing by Edamam for anything unusual
• Fullscreen barcode scanner with a triple product database
  (Open Food Facts + legacy + UPCItemDB) — Indian products included
• Nutrition-label photo reader (on-device OCR)
• Voice logging, full macros plus 13 micronutrients
• 1,000+ healthy USDA MyPlate Kitchen recipes, loggable in one tap

TRAINING THAT PUSHES YOU
• Structured weekly splits with form cues, GIFs, and rest timers
• Customize any day's exercises in HQ → Training Setup
• Search and add moves from the 700+ exercise library (wger)
• Full-screen rest timer with haptics
• Session completion celebrations, PR board with estimated 1RM
• Cardio + abs circuit tracking, steps and sleep burn
• Find gyms and parks near you (OpenStreetMap)

DAD COINS
Earn by doing — daily check-in, workouts, protein, calories, hydration,
weigh-ins, photos, and streak milestones. Never by simply opening the app.

PRIVACY FIRST
Continue Offline keeps everything on your device. Optional Google Sign-In
enables end-to-end encrypted cloud backup with a passphrase only you know.

PROGRESS YOU CAN SEE
Weight trends, before/after photo compare, consistency heatmap, and strength
records — all free, no subscription wall.
