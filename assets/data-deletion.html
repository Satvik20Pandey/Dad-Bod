Dad Bod Account and Data Deletion Request

Last updated: August 4, 2026

You can request deletion of your Dad Bod signed-in profile and any associated cloud backup data by contacting us. Dad Bod also supports self-service local deletion directly from your device.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email from the Google account used in Dad Bod, if you signed in with Google.
  2. Use subject line: Delete my Dad Bod account data.
  3. Include your name and the email address used in the app.
  4. If you also want any encrypted cloud backup blob removed, mention that in the request.
Email Deletion Request

What Gets Deleted

  • Profile details associated with a signed-in Dad Bod account.
  • Encrypted cloud backup data stored for that account, if backup was enabled and requested for deletion.
  • Locally stored fitness data on your device can also be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

What Stays on Your Device Unless You Remove It

Dad Bod is designed to work offline. Meal logs, workout logs, weight entries, hydration, custom training setup, and progress photos are primarily stored on your device.

If you use Continue Offline and never enable cloud backup, there may be no remote account data for us to delete beyond any support emails you send.

Retention and Processing Time

We typically process deletion requests within 7 business days.

Some data may remain temporarily in local device backups controlled by your device or platform provider until those backups rotate.

Self-Service Local Deletion

You can remove locally stored Dad Bod data immediately by clearing app storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app. This is the fastest way to erase on-device data.
