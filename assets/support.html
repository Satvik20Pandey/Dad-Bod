Common Issues

  • Online food search, barcodes, recipes, and nearby places need an internet connection. Logging and the Indian food dataset work offline.
  • If barcode or voice features do not start, check camera and microphone permissions in Android settings.
  • Nearby Gyms & Parks needs location permission, requested only when you open the feature.
  • If workout, weight, or meal data looks missing, confirm the selected date first.
  • If you use Google Sign-In, encrypted cloud backup is optional. Continue Offline keeps your tracking local to the device.
  • If your training plan looks different than expected, check Dad Bod HQ → Training Setup for the closed day, split start day, and custom day exercises.
  • If app behavior looks stale after an update, fully close and reopen the app.

What Dad Bod Supports

  • Meal logging, calorie and macro tracking, and nutrition label scan support.
  • Workout logging, custom per-day exercise plans, rest timers, and exercise guidance.
  • Body weight, progress photos, consistency tracking, and Dad Coins.
  • Encrypted cloud backup for signed-in users and local-only tracking for offline users.
