Common Issues

  • Online food search, barcodes, recipes, and nearby places need an internet connection — logging and the Indian food dataset work fully offline.
  • If barcode or voice features do not start, check camera/microphone permissions in Android settings.
  • Nearby Gyms & Parks needs location permission, requested only when you open the feature.
  • If workout or diet data looks missing, confirm the selected date.
  • If app behavior is stale after an update, close and reopen the app.
