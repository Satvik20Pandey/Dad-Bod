Dad Bod Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

1. Who We Are

Dad Bod is a fitness and nutrition tracking app published by Satvik Pandey. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the app.

2. Information We Process

  • Profile details you enter, such as name and email.
  • Fitness and health entries you create, such as meals, workouts, weight, hydration, and photos.
  • Optional voice input if you use voice meal logging (processed by your device's speech service).
  • Your approximate location, only while you use the optional "Nearby Gyms & Parks" feature.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide nutrition, workout, hydration, and progress-tracking functionality.
  • To generate scores, summaries, and charts in the app.
  • To answer the specific lookups you trigger (food search, barcode, recipes, nearby places).

4. Where Data Is Stored

Core app data is stored locally on your device (for example, browser/app local storage). Dad Bod does not run a mandatory central account server for core tracking features.

5. Third-Party Services

Dad Bod contacts public nutrition and fitness APIs only for the features you use, sending the minimum data needed and no personal identifiers:

  • Edamam — the food text you search online, for nutrition parsing.
  • Open Food Facts — barcodes you scan, for packaged-food data.
  • UPCItemDB — barcodes not found on Open Food Facts, for product-name lookup only.
  • MyPlate.food — age, sex, height, weight, and activity level for USDA calculators; recipe search terms.
  • wger.de — exercise names you search in the library.
  • Overpass / OpenStreetMap — your approximate coordinates when you open Nearby, to list gyms and parks. Location is never used in the background.
  • Tenor / media hosts — exercise demonstration GIFs.

Those providers process requests according to their own privacy policies. Review their policies before using those features.

6. Data Sharing and Selling

Dad Bod does not sell your personal data. We do not share personal information with data brokers or advertisers for targeted advertising.

7. Children’s Privacy

Dad Bod is not intended for children under 13 without parent or guardian supervision and consent. If you believe a child provided personal data without permission, contact us and we will help remove it from the device workflow where possible.

8. Your Choices and Data Deletion

  • You can edit or delete entries directly in the app.
  • You can clear app data in your device settings.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data.

For formal deletion requests, use: Account and Data Deletion Request

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. No method of storage or transmission is fully guaranteed to be secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
